What We Do

Core Engineering Services

Comprehensive Mechanical & Electrical capabilities delivered with precision and safety across Singapore.

Structural Fabrication

Structural material supply, custom steel fabrication, and Installation for building construction, Oil & Gas, and power plants.

Piping Solutions

Piping material supply, Prefabrication, site Installation and Hydrostatic Testing Services.

Plant Maintenance

Reliable scheduled plant shutdowns, heat exchanger overhauls, flange torque tensioning, and 24/7 rapid-response emergency leak interventions.

Electrical & Instruments

End-to-end electrical power distribution, Single Line Diagram (SLD) endorsement, sub-main cable pulling, MCC panels, and ACMV systems.

Fire Alarm & ELV

Installation of SCDF-compliant addressable fire alarms, ELV networks, CCTV security systems, and hazardous Ex-d sensor loops.

Skilled Manpower

Provision of certified 6G welders (GTAW/SMAW), electrical fitters, pipe fitters, and Licensed Electrical Workers for specialized site installations.
